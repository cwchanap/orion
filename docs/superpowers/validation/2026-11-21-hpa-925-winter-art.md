# HPA-925 winter art validation

## Generation and fitting

The images were generated with Codex's **built-in image generator**, as explicitly requested for this work. The tool did not expose a model identifier. Haruto's shipped HPA-924 chibi was supplied as the visual reference for the enemy and boss candidates. Other images were generated from the prompts below. No API key or external generator was used.

The 26 files remain at their original paths. Scenes were center-cropped and resampled to the existing sizes. Crests retain generated alpha. The three combat atlases were assembled from generated source cells with padding, retaining the original tower cells. The path source's two lower corner cells were reordered to match `GamePathTile`; their edges were blended to a shared snow/road profile and fitted into 444-pixel cells. The other art was not redrawn by the fitting process.

### Prompt set

- **Boss atlas:** "Create one transparent game sprite atlas for Last Hearth, matching the provided chibi reference's crisp anime line art, cel shading, proportions and three-quarter perspective. Seven DISTINCT full-body Frostbitten zombie bosses, eerie frozen blue-white and non-gory, each isolated centered in its own cell with generous transparent padding, no overlaps. Strict equal 4 columns by 2 rows. In reading order: hulking breach breaker, ice-shell matriarch, swarm mother, scrap-armored giant, regrowing warden, walker carrier, final frost-heart boss, completely empty transparent. Strong distinct silhouettes readable at 70 pixels. No text, borders, gridlines, backdrop, ground shadows, violence, gore or extra characters."
- **Walker, brute, breach and Hearth:** individual transparent isolated sprites using the Haruto reference; "Match the supplied chibi reference's proportions, clean crisp anime outlines and cel-shading. Three-quarter view, readable at 38 pixels, strong compact silhouette, cold winter blue and white with restrained warm accents. Transparent background, centered full subject with generous empty margin. Eerie and frozen, no gore, no text, no frame, no scenery or ground shadow." Subjects were a gaunt Frostbitten walker, a bulky ice-encrusted brute, a breach in a snow and ice wall, and an orange flame in a metal-and-stone brazier.
- **Effects:** a transparent 4-by-3 sheet, in order: warm light tracer, fireball, ice shard, warm hit spark, frost burst, streaking arrow/sniper round, lightning arc, serum cloud, gravity distortion, scrap drone, ice shell and scrap armor plate. Requested crisp tiny-game silhouettes, anime cel shading, transparent padding and no text or gridlines. A separate 2-by-2 sheet requested regrowth, corrosion and two distinct style-neutral signature glyphs.
- **Path:** "A strict equal 4 by 2 atlas of eight top-down square snow-road tiles for a winter tower defense game. Reading order: horizontal straight, vertical straight, north-east corner, north-west corner, south-east corner, south-west corner, start cap, end cap. Each road of packed darker blue-white snow meets relevant tile edges at the exact midpoint with consistent width, every background identical pale snowy texture, edge-to-edge and seamless. No outlines, no text, no margins, no gridlines. Anime cel shaded, simple legible game terrain." The first candidate's gridlines and swapped lower corners were corrected during fitting; the subsequently generated edit was not used.
- **Backdrops:** separate prompts for a warm command hall with hearth, overhead Hokkaido campaign map, lived-in camp shelter, quiet debrief room, and camp archive/map room. Each requested "Game UI backdrop for Last Hearth, anime illustration, clean crisp line art, cel-shaded, cold blue winter outside and warm orange camp light, cohesive grounded Hokkaido survival atmosphere," with quiet overlay space and no people, text, frames or UI.
- **Stages:** separate square illustrations of Ember Road, Frozen Rail Yard, Sunken Depot, Dead Foundry, Aurora Gate, White Bastion and The Cold Heart. Each requested "Anime cel shading, crisp line art, atmospheric cold blues and snow with restrained warm orange accents, coherent survival setting. Keep focal subject in middle horizontal band so square and 1.6:1 center crops both work. No people, lettering, labels, frames, UI or watermark."
- **Crests:** seven separate transparent icons of an ember road, snow-covered locomotive, buried depot, frozen foundry, mountain gate, bastion and crystalline heart. Each requested a centered compact silhouette readable at 30 pixels, crisp anime line art and cel shading, with no letters, borders or background.
- **Board and results:** the board prompt requested a strictly orthographic overhead, mid-bright, quiet snow field without a horizon, roads, objects or grid. The two wide result prompts requested a surviving warm camp and a fading camp hearth in a storm, respectively, in anime cel-shaded winter art without text or UI.

## Evidence and checks

- [Before and after fixture images](hpa-925-evidence/) are generated by `ORION_CAPTURE_DIR` using the existing Flutter renderer. The all-boss strip uses `OrionArt.boss` and `GameBalance.bosses` at 70 px. [Atlas and stage crops](hpa-925-evidence/atlases-and-stage-crops.jpg), [source path joins](hpa-925-evidence/path-joins.png), and [sprite-scale comparison](hpa-925-evidence/sprite-scale.png) are supporting source previews, not browser screenshots.
- The real-asset contract loads all 26 registered assets through Flutter, checks dimensions and integer atlas cells, full-bleed opacity, sprite-atlas gutters and the transparent eighth boss cell. The existing character contract uses shared decode helpers. A pixel comparison against baseline confirms the three core and five variety tower fallback cells are unchanged.
- The fixture battle shows snow beneath the existing dark cyan lane. The legacy lane, title/copy and the Camp backdrop's runtime stretching are HPA-926 scope. The approved HPA-921 mockup shows the current cyan lane and chibi presentation; this art pack retains that renderer while changing the underlying winter imagery. HPA-924's actual Haruto/Mio sprites were used for scale comparison.
- `flutter analyze --no-pub`, the full `flutter test --no-pub` suite (1,981 tests), and `flutter build web --release --no-pub` passed locally. The final full suite and analysis ran after the boss-strip font and loading assertions were corrected.
